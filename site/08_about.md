This is a non-commercial website dedicated to a scientific project
focusing on the development of a future complex plasma facility.

## The COMPACT Project

The future complex plasma facility COMPACT will allow the investigation of
large three-dimensional complex plasmas under microgravity conditions aboard
a space station.

## Technical Information & Website

This website serves as a platform to present the research and progress of the COMPACT facility.
