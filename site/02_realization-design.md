The design of COMPACT ensures the feasibility of the scientific research questions and consits of following main components: 

* Zyflex plasma chamber with following properties
    * Large chamber volume
    * Variable electrode distance
    * Low minimum gas pressure
* 4-channel RF generator
* Multiple particle dispensers
* Particle imaging of a thin particle slice (2D imaging system)
* Particle imaging in a subvolume of the dust cloud (3D imaging system)
* Plasma glow imaging
* Laser particle manipulation
* UV particle manipulation
