    * Variable electrode distance
    * Low minimum gas pressure
* 4-channel RF generator
* Multiple particle dispensers
* Particle imaging of a thin particle slice (2D imaging system)
* Particle imaging in a subvolume of the dust cloud (3D imaging system)

# Plasma glow imaging

The plasma glow imaging system will capture the glow emission from the argon plasma. The most intense argon (double) spectral lines are near 750 nm, 763 nm, 810 nm and 840 nm. A hyperspectral camera is used to capture the spatially resolved plasma glow. The envisaged hyperspectral camera has a 2 MPix chip with deposited Fabry-Perot filters in 5 x 5 arrays. Thereby, 25 spectral bands in the range from about 650 nm to 950 nm are simultaneously recorded with a spatial resolution of about 400 x 250 pixels with a frame rate of about 50 fps. From this spectral, time and space resolved data information on the plasma properties can be derived, such as (relative) plasma density or temperature.
