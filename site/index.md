# COMPACT

COMPACT (**Com**plex **P**l**a**sma Fa**c**ili**t**y) is a multi-user facility for the invesitgation of complex (dusty) plasmas under microgravity conditions aboard a space station.






The future complex plasma facility
[COMPACT](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6587/ac9ff0)

under . COMPACT is a project
with international scientific contributions, supported by space agencies
(DLR, NASA, ESA) and NSF.
