# Privacy Policy and Data Protection Information

We process personal data that is collected when our website is visited in
accordance with the valid data protection laws and regulations, in particular
the EU’s General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the
*Landesdatenschutzgesetz* - DSG M-V
(State Data Protection Act of Mecklenburg-Vorpommern).

This is a non-commercial website dedicated to a scientific project focusing on the development of the complex plasma facility COMPACT.

## Hosting

This website is hosted internally by the University Computer Centre of the
University of Greifswald. The technical infrastructure is operated directly by
the university. No external third-party hosting provider is involved in the
storage and provision of the website content.

Note: The source code is built on GitLab.com via CI/CD pipelines, but the
published static website contains no external connections to GitLab.com or
other third-party services.

## Legal Basis

If you contact us via GitLab Issues or email, your data (such as your username
or email address, if provided) is processed based on your consent pursuant to
Article 6(1) lit. a GDPR for the purpose of responding to your inquiry.

## Log Files

The following data is stored in log files every time a visitor accesses our
website for reasons related to data security and for statistical purposes:

* IP address,
* time the website was accessed,
* user agent (browser and operating system),
* visited pages or content elements, and
* referrer URL, if provided (page visited previously).

The purpose of logging is to recognise, limit and eliminate malfunctions and
to detect misuse in accordance with § 12(1) and (4) TDDDG. The IP addresses
will not be merged with other data sets. The data will be stored for a maximum
of seven days, after which they will be stored in anonymous form for website
statistics. Our webspace uses the open-source software AWStats for website
statistics. No personal data is stored.

## No Cookies or Tracking

This website does not collect any data.
This website does not use cookies, tracking tools, or analytics services.
No personal data is collected or stored beyond the server log files described
above.

## Your Rights

You are entitled to withdraw your consent for the processing of personal data
at any time and without having to provide reasons, with effect for the future,
by writing to the
University of Greifswald, Domstraße 11, DE-17489 Greifswald, Germany,
or via email to
<a href="mailto:hochschulkommunikation@uni-greifswald.de">hochschulkommunikation@uni-greifswald.de</a>.

You also have the right to receive information upon request and free of charge
about your stored personal data, its origin and recipient, the purpose, and
the length of data processing. Furthermore, under certain circumstances, you
have the right to the correction, limitation of the processing (e.g. blocking),
deletion, and the data portability of your data.

In addition, you also have the right to submit a complaint to the responsible
supervisory authority for data protection, if, in your opinion, your details
are not being processed in a lawful manner.
